# NAMEWISE / FACE RECALL — PRIVACY POLICY

**Effective Date:** July 12, 2026
**Last Updated:** November 5, 2026

## Introduction

Namewise was previously called Face Recall. Sections 1–5 below describe the
Apple App Store version. Section 6 describes the Android version distributed
through Google Play and its platform-specific purchase services.

Face Recall is a local-first memory game. It lets you practice with bundled
face images and optionally create local custom packs from photos selected on
your device.

Contact: **cankatsarac@gmail.com**

## 1. INFORMATION WE HANDLE

### 1.1 Stored locally on your device

- Custom face-pack images that you select, plus the names or labels you enter.
- Profiles, sessions, scores, streaks, achievements, study progress, and reports.
- Preferences, purchase state, and generated reports or share cards.

Custom images are cached and used on-device. The current code does not upload
them to us or to an AI service. The system Photos picker controls what you make
available to the App; we do not need broad access to your entire photo library.

### 1.2 Speech and audio

The App uses Apple's on-device speech synthesizer to speak names during practice.
It does not record or transcribe your voice, and it does not request microphone
access.

### 1.3 Apple services

If you use Game Center, Apple processes your Game Center identity, scores, and
achievements. If you buy Face Recall Pro, Apple processes payment, transaction,
and subscription information. We receive only the purchase entitlement needed
to unlock features. Apple's own privacy policy applies to those services.

### 1.4 Information we do not collect

We do not operate an account system or backend and do not collect your name,
email, contacts, location, camera recordings, microphone recordings,
advertising identifier, or analytics data. The current version has no ads,
third-party analytics SDK, Firebase, Supabase, or cloud AI service.

## 2. SHARING

When you choose to export a report or share card, the App uses Apple's system
share sheet. The service or person you select receives the content. We do not
control that recipient's use of it.

## 3. CHILDREN AND SENSITIVE IMAGES

Face Recall is a general-audience game, not a face-recognition or identity
verification service. Do not add a person's image without the necessary
permission. Do not use the App to make decisions about another person. The App
is not directed to children under 13 (or the applicable local age).

## 4. RETENTION, DELETION, AND RIGHTS

Local images, profiles, and progress remain until you remove them, reset the
App, or uninstall it. You can revoke Photos access in Apple Settings. Apple
retains store and Game Center records under its own policies. EEA, UK, and
California residents may contact us about applicable privacy rights.

## 6. NAMEWISE FOR ANDROID / GOOGLE PLAY

This section applies to the Android app with package `com.teampire.namewise`.
It replaces the Apple-specific service and storage descriptions above for
that version. The same controller and contact details in section 5 apply.

### Local practice data and device features

Profiles, names or labels you enter, practice history, progress, preferences,
custom face packs and reports are stored in the app's private Android storage.
The app uses Android's system photo picker to import only photos you select;
it does not request broad photo-library, camera or microphone access. Imported
photos and names are not sent to the developer, RevenueCat or an AI service.
Pronunciation uses installed offline Android text-to-speech voices; if a
suitable offline voice is unavailable, speech is unavailable. The app does
not record your voice or perform face recognition or identity verification.
Optional reminders run locally with your permission. Home-screen widgets use
committed progress summaries without publishing your local profile names or
custom photos to widget storage. Android cloud backup is disabled for the app.

The app has no login account, advertising SDK, cloud AI or developer-operated
gameplay backend. Local names and gameplay history are not purchase identifiers.

### Google Play and RevenueCat purchases

Google Play processes payments, transactions and subscriptions under
[Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). We do not receive
your payment-card details or Google Account password.

The Android app uses RevenueCat to validate purchases, maintain Pro access,
restore purchases and provide subscription reporting. Its SDK may connect at
app launch before you buy anything. RevenueCat processes an app-generated
anonymous purchase identifier, purchase history, transaction and entitlement
records, and technical metadata such as app/SDK/device versions, locale,
network information and the time it was contacted. The developer can view
purchase and subscription records in RevenueCat. These services support app
functionality and subscription analytics, not targeted advertising or tracking
across apps. We do not send custom photos, local profile names, practice scores
or report contents to RevenueCat. See [RevenueCat's privacy policy](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy).

### Optional complimentary access codes

Android releases that display **Redeem access code** can apply a complimentary
code without creating an account or making a purchase. When you choose this
action, the app sends the code and its anonymous RevenueCat purchase identifier
over HTTPS to our access service hosted on Cloudflare Workers. Cloudflare also
receives the network metadata needed to handle the request, such as your IP
address. This service checks the code and asks RevenueCat to grant Pro access
to the existing anonymous Android purchase record. It does not receive local
names, photos, practice history, reports, Google credentials or payment details.

The service stores code hashes rather than redeemable codes. Request-body and
application request logging are disabled. A hash of the connecting IP address
is held temporarily in server memory for abuse prevention; the service does
not write it to a database. Cloudflare handles its own service and security
records under [Cloudflare's privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/).
RevenueCat retains the resulting entitlement record to provide access. The
access-code service is for app functionality and abuse prevention, not
advertising. The purchase-record deletion contact below also covers these
complimentary entitlement records. Clearing app storage may replace the
anonymous identifier; an eligible reusable code can be applied again.

### Sharing, retention and deletion requests

A report or image leaves the app when you choose to share it through Android's
system share sheet. Your selected recipient handles that copy. Delete local
custom packs or photos in the app, or clear Android app storage or uninstall
to remove local app data. Manage separately any copies you shared elsewhere.

Uninstalling does not cancel a Google Play subscription or delete purchase
records held by Google or RevenueCat. Manage subscriptions in Google Play.
To request deletion of developer-controlled purchase records, email
**cankatsarac@gmail.com** with the subject **Namewise Android data deletion**.
We may ask for information needed to locate the anonymous purchase record and
verify your request. Do not send payment-card details or account passwords.
Legal retention obligations and Google's own records may limit deletion.

The Android app is intended for adults aged 18 and older and is not directed
to children. This Android audience replaces the age description above for the
Google Play version. Obtain permission before adding someone's image. The
practice game provides no medical diagnosis or treatment.
