# FACE RECALL — PRIVACY POLICY

**Effective Date:** July 12, 2026
**Last Updated:** July 12, 2026

## Introduction

Face Recall is a local-first memory game. It lets you practice with bundled
face images and optionally create local custom packs from photos selected on
your device.

Contact: **cankatsarac@gmail.com**

## 1. INFORMATION WE HANDLE

### 1.1 Stored locally on your device

- Custom face-pack images that you select, plus the names or labels you enter.
- Profiles, sessions, scores, streaks, achievements, study progress, and reports.
- Preferences, purchase state, and generated reports or share cards.

Custom images are cached and used on-device. The current code does not upload
them to us or to an AI service. The system Photos picker controls what you make
available to the App; we do not need broad access to your entire photo library.

### 1.2 Speech and audio

The App uses Apple's on-device speech synthesizer to speak names during practice.
It does not record or transcribe your voice, and it does not request microphone
access.

### 1.3 Apple services

If you use Game Center, Apple processes your Game Center identity, scores, and
achievements. If you buy Face Recall Pro, Apple processes payment, transaction,
and subscription information. We receive only the purchase entitlement needed
to unlock features. Apple's own privacy policy applies to those services.

### 1.4 Information we do not collect

We do not operate an account system or backend and do not collect your name,
email, contacts, location, camera recordings, microphone recordings,
advertising identifier, or analytics data. The current version has no ads,
third-party analytics SDK, Firebase, Supabase, or cloud AI service.

## 2. SHARING

When you choose to export a report or share card, the App uses Apple's system
share sheet. The service or person you select receives the content. We do not
control that recipient's use of it.

## 3. CHILDREN AND SENSITIVE IMAGES

Face Recall is a general-audience game, not a face-recognition or identity
verification service. Do not add a person's image without the necessary
permission. Do not use the App to make decisions about another person. The App
is not directed to children under 13 (or the applicable local age).

## 4. RETENTION, DELETION, AND RIGHTS

Local images, profiles, and progress remain until you remove them, reset the
App, or uninstall it. You can revoke Photos access in Apple Settings. Apple
retains store and Game Center records under its own policies. EEA, UK, and
California residents may contact us about applicable privacy rights.
