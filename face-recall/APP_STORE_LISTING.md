## Description

Face Recall is a thoughtful memory practice game for learning names and
recognizing visual details.

Study a face pack, test yourself in focused rounds, and build a stronger recall
habit over time. Use the built-in coach, streaks, trends, and reports to make
practice feel structured without turning it into a judgment.

Highlights:

- Study and recall faces across several practice modes
- Use typed recall and focused test rounds
- Create custom face packs from photos selected on your device
- Keep separate local profiles for a household
- Track sessions, streaks, achievements, and your own recall trends
- Hear names spoken aloud with Apple's speech synthesizer
- Export a progress report or share card using Apple's share sheet
- Keep custom images and gameplay history on your device
- Optional Game Center achievements and leaderboards
- Optional Face Recall Pro upgrades through Apple

Face Recall is not facial recognition, identity verification, or a medical,
psychological, or cognitive diagnosis. Your results are practice feedback and
can vary from day to day.

No account is required. Custom photos are not uploaded by the current version,
and the app does not use advertising or third-party analytics SDKs.
