# CHRONICLE PRIVACY POLICY

**Effective Date:** June 14, 2026
**Last Updated:** June 14, 2026

## Introduction

Welcome to Chronicle ("we," "our," or "us"). We respect your privacy and have designed this app with a privacy-first philosophy. This Privacy Policy explains how Chronicle handles information when you use our mobile application ("App"), and — importantly — what we do **not** collect.

Chronicle is a single-player, offline history timeline trivia game. You place historical events in chronological order, build daily streaks, earn experience and achievements, and track your own statistics. The App is designed so that everything you do stays **on your device**, tied to no account, under no login, linked to no profile. The App does not require an internet connection to play.

## 1. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

### 1.1 No Account, No Profile

Chronicle **does not require you to create an account**. We do not collect:

- Your name
- Your email address
- Your phone number
- Your username or social handles
- Your date of birth
- Your photo, avatar, or biographical information
- Any contact list, calendar, photo library, location, or social graph

There is no login screen because there is no account.

### 1.2 Data Stored on Your Device

As you play, the App saves the following **only on your device**, in your device's sandboxed local storage, and never transmits it to us or anyone else:

- Your game history and recent rounds
- Your statistics (best score, accuracy, total rounds, total correct placements)
- Your daily streak, longest streak, and last-played dates
- Your experience points (XP), level, and tier
- Your unlocked achievements
- Your per-category accuracy and category streaks
- Your progress on the Chronicle Pass
- Whether you have seen the tutorial
- Your detected or selected language preference

If you delete the App — or use the in-app **"Reset Ledger"** option — this data is deleted with it.

### 1.3 No Purchases, No Advertising

Chronicle is **free** and contains **no in-app purchases, no subscriptions, and no advertising**. We do not collect payment information, billing data, or purchase identifiers, and we do not integrate any advertising network.

### 1.4 Automatically Collected Information

We do **not** run any third-party analytics SDK, advertising SDK, attribution SDK, or crash-reporting tool that transmits identifiers about you. We do **not** collect:

- Device advertising identifier (IDFA / AAID)
- Your IP address
- Precise or approximate geolocation
- App usage analytics
- Crash or performance telemetry
- Clickstream data

Chronicle is built using the **Rork development toolkit**. In Chronicle's shipped configuration its optional analytics component is **disabled**, so no usage data is collected or transmitted to Rork or any third party.

### 1.5 No Network Connection Required

Chronicle functions fully offline. Its event content ships inside the App, and the App does not operate or contact any first-party server. It does not send your gameplay, statistics, or any other information anywhere.

## 2. HOW WE USE YOUR INFORMATION

The on-device data described in Section 1.2 is used solely to:

- Run the game and track your progress within a round
- Store your statistics, streaks, achievements, XP, and history so you can return to them in your Ledger
- Provide the daily challenge and Chronicle Pass
- Show the tutorial once and remember your language preference

We do **not** use your information for marketing, advertising, profiling, training AI models, or any purpose beyond operating the App on your device.

## 3. INFORMATION SHARING AND DISCLOSURE

### 3.1 We Do Not Sell Your Data

We do not sell, rent, or trade any data — personal or otherwise — to any party. We do not share data with advertisers, data brokers, or marketers. Because your data never leaves your device, there is, in practice, nothing for us to sell or share.

### 3.2 Service Providers

We do not transmit your information to any processor. The only third parties involved are the platforms that distribute the App:

- **Apple** — for distribution through the App Store (governed by Apple's privacy policy)
- **Google** — for distribution through the Google Play Store (governed by Google's privacy policy)

Apple and Google may independently collect download counts, crash data, and device information under their own policies as part of operating their stores. That collection is outside our control and is described in their respective privacy policies.

### 3.3 Legal Requirements

We may disclose information if required by law, legal process, or a valid government request, or to protect our rights and property, prevent illegal activities, or ensure user safety. Because your data never leaves your device, there is typically nothing for us to disclose in response to such a request.

### 3.4 Business Transfers

If Chronicle is involved in a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, any information we hold (which is minimal) may be transferred as part of that transaction. You will be notified of any such change and of any resulting changes to this Privacy Policy.

## 4. DATA SECURITY

We implement privacy-by-design and industry-standard safeguards:

- On-device data is stored in the operating system's sandboxed storage, protected by the device's disk encryption
- The App does not operate a backend database of user content; there is no server-side store to breach
- We regularly review our dependencies for security advisories

However, no method of electronic storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 5. DATA RETENTION

Because we do not store your content on any server, **retention is controlled by you**:

- Your statistics, progress, achievements, and preferences live on your device until you delete the App or clear its data in your device settings
- You can also erase all in-app progress at any time using the **"Reset Ledger"** feature

To stop all local retention immediately, uninstall the App.

## 6. YOUR RIGHTS AND CHOICES

Depending on where you live, you may have the following rights:

- **Access** — request information about what data we hold about you (in practice, none, because your data stays on your device)
- **Rectification** — correct information we hold about you
- **Erasure** — request deletion of any personal information we hold about you
- **Restriction** — restrict how we process your information
- **Portability** — request a copy of your data in a structured, commonly used format
- **Objection** — object to processing of your information
- **Withdrawal of consent** — withdraw any consent you have given

Because Chronicle holds no personal information about you on any server, you can exercise the most important of these rights — erasure — directly and immediately by using "Reset Ledger" or uninstalling the App. For any other request, email us at **cankatsarac@gmail.com**.
