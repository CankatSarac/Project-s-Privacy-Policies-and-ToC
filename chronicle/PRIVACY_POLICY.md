# CHRONICLE — PRIVACY POLICY

**Effective Date:** May 15, 2026
**Last Updated:** May 15, 2026

---

## Introduction

Chronicle is a mobile application that delivers themed packs of historical timeline trivia, with daily challenges, streaks, achievements, and a weekly tournament. Premium content is offered through a paid subscription.

Contact: **cankatsarac@gmail.com**

---

## 1. WHAT WE COLLECT

### 1.1 Stored locally on your device

- Your pack progress, completed challenges, scores, and streak count.
- Achievement state and tournament results.
- Onboarding state, notification opt-in flag, and app preferences.
- Bookmarks and saved facts.

### 1.2 Subscription state

If you subscribe to Chronicle Pass, RevenueCat receives transaction identifiers, subscription status, platform identifiers, and an anonymous RevenueCat user identifier. RevenueCat does not receive any in-app content.

### 1.3 Information we do NOT collect

- No account, no login.
- No name, email, phone number, contacts, photos, microphone, or camera.
- No location data despite some related libraries appearing in our dependencies.
- No advertising SDKs or advertising identifiers.
- No third-party analytics SDKs.
- No backend operated by us beyond RevenueCat for entitlement.
- We do not sell, rent, or trade data.

| Data | Legal basis |
|---|---|
| Locally stored progress | Held on your device by you; we do not access. |

---

## 4. NOTIFICATIONS

If you opt in, Chronicle schedules local notifications on your device for daily challenges and streak reminders. There is no push notification server we operate; nothing is sent off-device for notification delivery.

---

## 5. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

Chronicle is suitable for ages 13+ (16+ in the EEA without parental consent). Historical content can include topics that may not be suitable for very young children. Parental discretion is advised. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

---

## 6. YOUR RIGHTS

- **Access:** All data is on your device.
- **Delete:** Uninstall the app or use the in-app reset.
- **EEA / UK:** lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority.
- **California (CCPA / CPRA):** know, delete, correct, opt-out of sale/sharing (we do not), limit sensitive PI use, non-discrimination.
