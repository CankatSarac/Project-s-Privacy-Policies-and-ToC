## 2. DESCRIPTION OF SERVICE

Chronicle is a single-player history timeline trivia game. The App allows you to:

- Place historical events into their correct chronological order on a timeline
- Play a daily challenge and build daily streaks
- Choose between difficulty modes (Eras, Decades, and Same Year)
- Quick-play by category or launch curated themed packs
- Earn experience points (XP), levels, tiers, and achievements
- Track your statistics and per-category mastery in your personal Ledger

The App is **free**, requires **no account**, and runs **fully offline**. All of your progress is stored on your own device.
