## 3. DESCRIPTION OF SERVICE

Witness lets you:

- Capture a single selfie that an AI image model ages.
- Record a ten-second voice sample that is processed locally to choose a premade older voice.
- Have a short conversation with the aged portrait, in voice and text.
- Optionally provide a short bio and social-handle context (Pro) so the older self can speak to who you actually are.
- Optionally subscribe to Witness Pro to unlock extended capacity and additional features.

Witness does not run a social network. The conversation is not shared with anyone else.
