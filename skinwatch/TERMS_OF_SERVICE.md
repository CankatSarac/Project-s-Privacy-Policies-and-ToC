### 2.2 Screening signal only

The output the App produces — ABCDE scores, a risk category, a confidence value, a headline, and recommendations — is a **screening signal designed to help you decide whether to consult a dermatologist**. It is not a diagnosis, prognosis, or determination of malignancy. The model can be wrong in either direction:

- It may misclassify a benign lesion as concerning ("over-referral").
- It may misclassify a concerning lesion as benign ("false reassurance"). **False reassurance is the more dangerous error.**

### 2.3 Single-timepoint and no-scale limitations

A single smartphone photograph cannot establish **Evolution** (change over time) or reliable **Diameter** (without a scale reference). The App's confidence values reflect these limitations. Even a "low risk" output does not rule out melanoma or other skin cancer.

### 2.4 You must consult a clinician

**If you have any concern about a skin lesion — change in size, shape, color, bleeding, itching, or new lesion — see a qualified dermatologist or physician promptly, regardless of the SkinWatch output.** The App is intended to lower the barrier to seeking professional care, not to replace it.
