# SEDIMENT — PRIVACY POLICY

**Effective Date:** July 12, 2026
**Last Updated:** July 12, 2026

## Introduction

Sediment is a local-first reflective game and breathing/attention companion.
Gameplay, observations, and time capsules are stored on your Apple device.

Contact: **cankatsarac@gmail.com**

## 1. INFORMATION WE HANDLE

### 1.1 Stored locally

- Erosion progress, streaks, attention sessions, observations, and preferences.
- Time capsules, journal-like text, cosmetic unlocks, and local purchase state.
- Data shared between the App and its widget through Apple's on-device App Group.
- Live Activity state shown on your device when you enable a flood/live activity.

The App does not operate a backend and does not transmit this content to us.

### 1.2 Apple services

Apple processes payment, transaction, and subscription information for Sediment
Patron or Deep Time purchases. We receive only the entitlement state required
to unlock features. Apple may separately process information for App Store
distribution and any Game Center or device services you choose to use.

### 1.3 Information we do not collect

The current version has no account, login, advertising SDK, third-party
analytics, Firebase, Supabase, AI relay, or user-data backend. We do not collect
your name, email, contacts, location, photos, camera, microphone recordings,
or advertising identifier.

## 2. SHARING AND DEVICE FEATURES

If you export or share an almanac, card, or other material, Apple's system share
sheet sends it to the destination you choose. Widgets and Live Activities are
Apple device features; their display and system handling are governed by Apple.

## 3. CHILDREN AND WELLNESS DISCLAIMER

Sediment is a general-audience game and reflection tool, not medical advice,
therapy, or a treatment for any condition. It is not directed at children under
13 (or the applicable local age), and we do not knowingly collect children's
personal information.

## 4. RETENTION AND RIGHTS

Local data remains until you delete it in the App, reset it, or uninstall the
App. EEA, UK, and California residents may contact us about applicable privacy
rights. Apple retains store records under its own policies and legal duties.
