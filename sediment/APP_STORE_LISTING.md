## Description

Sediment is a reflective game about time, attention, and gradual change.

Watch a canyon evolve as you return to small moments of stillness. Notice what
changes, record observations, tend a streak, and place thoughts into time
capsules that can wait for another day. Sediment is designed to feel calm,
tactile, and rewarding without demanding constant attention.

Highlights:

- Shape an evolving canyon through daily attention
- Record observations and reflective notes locally
- Build a gentle tending streak
- Create and reveal time capsules
- Explore an almanac of your changing world
- Use optional widgets and Live Activities on your device
- Export an almanac or share card through Apple's share sheet
- Enjoy ambient rain sounds and quiet visual feedback
- Keep your content on your device with no account required
- Optional cosmetics and Deep Time features through Apple

Sediment is a game and reflection tool, not therapy, medical advice, or a
treatment for any condition. Your reflections belong to you, and you choose
whether to share them.

The current version has no advertising or third-party analytics SDK and no
user-data backend.
