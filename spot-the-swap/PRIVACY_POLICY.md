# SPOT THE SWAP — PRIVACY POLICY

**Effective Date:** July 12, 2026
**Last Updated:** July 12, 2026

## Introduction

Spot the Swap is a local-first visual comparison and brain-training game. It
does not operate a user account or application backend.

Contact: **cankatsarac@gmail.com**

## 1. INFORMATION WE HANDLE

### 1.1 Stored locally on your device

- Household profile names and emoji avatars that you create.
- Scores, coins, themes, boosters, streaks, achievements, and session history.
- Preferences and local notification choices.
- Reports, challenge codes, and share cards while prepared for sharing.

This information remains local unless you choose to share content through
Apple's system share sheet.

### 1.2 Optional Apple services

- **Game Center:** Apple processes your Game Center identity, leaderboard
  scores, and achievements if you use those features.
- **StoreKit:** Apple processes payment, transaction, and subscription data for
  Swap Pro and coin packs. We receive only the entitlement needed to provide
  purchased features.
- **Local notifications:** If you allow reminders, the App schedules them on
  your device. We do not operate a push-notification server.

Apple's privacy policy applies to Apple's services.

### 1.3 Information we do not collect

We do not collect names beyond the local profile label you enter, email,
contacts, location, photos, camera or microphone recordings, advertising
identifier, or browsing history. The current version has no advertising SDK,
third-party analytics SDK, Firebase, Supabase, AI relay, or user-data backend.

## 2. SHARING

Challenge codes, reports, text, and images are sent only when you invoke the
system share sheet. The recipient or service you select controls subsequent use.

## 3. CHILDREN

The game is for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13 (or
the applicable local age). We do not knowingly collect personal information
from children.

## 4. RETENTION AND YOUR RIGHTS

Local data remains until you delete it in the App, reset the App, or uninstall
it. Notification permission can be withdrawn in Apple Settings. Apple retains
store and Game Center records under its own policies. EEA, UK, and California
residents may contact us about applicable privacy rights.
