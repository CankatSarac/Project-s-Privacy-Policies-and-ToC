## Description

Spot the Swap is a fast visual comparison game built around focus, observation,
and repeatable daily practice.

Study a scene, find what changed, and make your move before the round ends.
Play quick sessions, build a streak, earn coins, unlock themes, and compare
your progress with your own history. When you want a rematch, share a challenge
code with a friend.

Highlights:

- Find visual swaps across colorful scene challenges
- Play daily workouts, practice rounds, and shared challenges
- Track scores, streaks, achievements, and Brain Score feedback
- Create separate local profiles for two people on one device
- Earn coins and unlock visual themes
- Share challenge codes, reports, and game cards with Apple's share sheet
- Turn on an optional local daily workout reminder
- Use optional Game Center leaderboards and achievements
- Optional Swap Pro subscription and coin packs through Apple
- Play without an account or user-data backend

Spot the Swap is an entertainment and practice game. Its scores and feedback
are not medical, psychological, or cognitive diagnoses, and they should not be
used to make decisions about another person.

The current version has no advertising or third-party analytics SDK.
