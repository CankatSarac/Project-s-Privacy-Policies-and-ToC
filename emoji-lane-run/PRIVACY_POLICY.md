# MIND THE LANE — PRIVACY POLICY

**Effective Date:** July 19, 2026

**Last Updated:** November 5, 2026

## 1. Overview

Mind the Lane (the **“App”**) is a local-first memory-practice game for iOS.
The App was previously developed under the working name Emoji Lane Run, which
is still used in some project and policy URLs.

The App does not require an account. Cankat Saraç (the **“Developer,” “we,”
“us,”** or **“our”**) does not operate an application backend and does not
receive your gameplay history or local profile names. Apple and RevenueCat process
purchase and technical information as described below.

This policy explains the information the App stores on your device, the limited
circumstances in which information can leave your device, and your choices.

## 2. Information Stored on Your Device

The App stores the following information in its private iOS app storage:

- Run history, including dates, scores, levels, duration, gates attempted,
  correct and incorrect choices, sequence length, and timing information.
- Personal progress derived from that history, including best scores, streaks,
  streak freezes, and trend calculations.
- App preferences, such as sound and haptic cue settings.
- The local profile name, avatar choice, and active profile selection where
  profile features are available.
- Result cards and reports generated temporarily when you choose to share them.

This information is used only to provide the App's gameplay, progress,
personalization, reporting, and sharing features. It is processed on your
device and is not transmitted to the Developer.

## 3. Information We Do Not Collect Through the App

The current version contains no developer-operated backend, advertising SDK,
advertising analytics, cross-app tracking, or AI service. RevenueCat provides purchase
validation, subscription reporting and hosted paywalls as described in section 4.3. Through the App,
we do not collect:

- Your contact details, precise or approximate location, contacts, photos, or
  camera or microphone recordings.
- Advertising identifiers, browsing history, search history, or data from
  other apps and websites.
- Your gameplay history, local profile information, preferences, or trend data.

We do not sell personal information, use it for targeted advertising, or track
you across apps or websites.

## 4. When Information Can Leave Your Device

### 4.1 Sharing you initiate

The App can create a result card or report from locally stored information. A
file leaves the App only when you choose the Share action and select a person,
app, or service through Apple's system share sheet. The selected recipient or
service will receive the content you choose to share and will handle it under
its own terms and privacy practices. We do not receive a copy and cannot delete
copies held by you or the selected recipient.

### 4.2 Apple services

Apple processes information needed to download the App and may process App
Store transaction or entitlement information under Apple's own privacy policy.
The Developer does not receive your payment-card details or Apple Account
password.

The version covered by this policy does not activate Game Center or submit
scores to a Game Center leaderboard. If a future version enables Game Center,
this policy and the App Store privacy information will be updated before that
data practice takes effect.

Apple's privacy policy is available at:
https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

### 4.3 RevenueCat purchase services

The App uses RevenueCat for purchase validation, entitlement management,
hosted purchase screens and subscription reporting. The SDK can contact
RevenueCat at app launch, before a purchase is made. It processes an
app-generated anonymous purchase identifier, transaction and entitlement
information, and technical information such as app/device versions, network
information and last-seen activity. The Developer can view purchase and
subscription status in RevenueCat. Local profile names and gameplay history
are not sent to RevenueCat. We do not use this information for targeted
advertising or cross-app tracking.

RevenueCat's privacy policy is available at:
https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

Deleting the App does not cancel subscriptions or erase records held by Apple
or RevenueCat. Contact the Developer about purchase-data rights requests;
provider retention and legal requirements may apply. Manage or cancel a
subscription through your Apple Account settings.

### 4.4 Support communications

If you email us for support, we receive your email address and any information
you include in the message. We use that information to respond, investigate the
issue, keep reasonable support records, and comply with legal obligations. Do
not include sensitive information that is not needed for your request. Our
email provider processes these communications on our behalf.

## 5. Retention and Deletion

App data remains in the App's private storage until iOS removes it or you
uninstall the App. Files prepared for sharing may remain in the App's temporary
storage until iOS clears them or the App is uninstalled. Uninstalling the App
removes its locally stored data from that device, subject to Apple's device and
backup behavior.

The Developer cannot retrieve or delete your on-device data because we do not
receive it. You can view available gameplay and progress information inside the
App. If you share a file, you must manage or delete the shared copy through the
recipient or service you selected.

Support emails are kept only for as long as reasonably needed to answer the
request, maintain support records, resolve disputes, or meet legal obligations.
Apple retains information it controls according to its own policies.

## 6. Security

On-device information is kept within the App's iOS storage container and is
protected by the security features of your device and iOS. No method of storage
is completely secure, so you should protect your device with a passcode and
keep iOS up to date.

## 7. Children

The App is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children
under 13 or the applicable minimum age in their country. The App does not
transmit gameplay or profile data to the Developer. A parent or guardian should
contact us on behalf of a child and should supervise any information the child
chooses to share outside the App.

## 8. Your Privacy Rights and Choices

Depending on where you live, privacy law may provide rights such as access,
correction, deletion, restriction, objection, or complaint to a data-protection
authority. Because the Developer does not receive or control your on-device App
data, we cannot identify, access, correct, or delete it remotely. You control
that data through the App, your device, and by uninstalling the App.

For personal information contained in a support communication, you may contact
us to exercise any rights that apply to you. We may need enough information to
verify and process the request.

## 9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when the App or applicable requirements change. We
will revise the **Last Updated** date above and, where appropriate, provide
additional notice in the App or through the App Store. We will update this
policy before introducing a materially different data practice.

## 11. Mind the Lane for Android / Google Play

This section extends the policy to the Android app with package
`com.teampire.mindthelane`. It replaces the iOS-specific storage and Apple
service descriptions above for that version; the same controller and contact
details in section 10 apply.

Run history, local runner profiles, progress, preferences and generated reports
remain in the app's private Android storage. Android cloud backup is disabled.
The Android version does not require an account or use a developer-operated
gameplay backend, advertising SDK, cloud AI, location, camera or microphone.
Home-screen widgets use committed progress summaries without publishing local
runner names or run history to widget storage. A report or image leaves the
app only when you choose to share it through Android's system share sheet.
The person or service you select handles that copy.

Google Play processes downloads, payments, transactions and subscriptions under
[Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). We do not receive
your payment-card details or Google Account password. The Android app uses
RevenueCat for purchase validation, Plus access, restores and subscription
reporting. Its SDK can connect at app launch before a purchase. RevenueCat
processes an app-generated anonymous purchase identifier, purchase history,
transaction and entitlement information, and technical metadata such as
app/SDK/device versions, locale, network information and the time it was
contacted. The developer can view purchase and subscription status in
RevenueCat. These services support app functionality and subscription analytics,
not targeted advertising or tracking across apps. Local runner names, gameplay
history and report contents are not sent to RevenueCat. See
[RevenueCat's privacy policy](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy).

Android releases that display **Redeem access code** can apply a complimentary
code without creating an account or making a purchase. When you choose this
action, the app sends the code and its anonymous RevenueCat purchase identifier
over HTTPS to our access service hosted on Cloudflare Workers. Cloudflare also
receives the network metadata needed to handle the request, such as your IP
address. This service checks the code and asks RevenueCat to grant Plus access
to the existing anonymous Android purchase record. It does not receive local
runner names, gameplay history, reports, Google credentials or payment details.

The service stores code hashes rather than redeemable codes. Request-body and
application request logging are disabled. A hash of the connecting IP address
is held temporarily in server memory for abuse prevention; the service does
not write it to a database. Cloudflare handles its own service and security
records under [Cloudflare's privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/).
RevenueCat retains the resulting entitlement record to provide access. The
access-code service is for app functionality and abuse prevention, not
advertising. The purchase-record deletion contact below also covers these
complimentary entitlement records. Clearing app storage may replace the
anonymous identifier; an eligible reusable code can be applied again.

Clear Android app storage or uninstall the app to remove local app data.
Manage separately any report or image copies you shared. Uninstalling does
not cancel subscriptions or erase purchase records held by Google or RevenueCat.
Manage or cancel subscriptions in Google Play. To request deletion of
developer-controlled purchase records, email **dev@teampire.uk** with the subject
**Mind the Lane Android data deletion**. We may request information needed to
locate the anonymous purchase record and verify your request. Do not send
payment-card details or account passwords. Legal retention obligations and
Google's own records may limit deletion.

The Android app is intended for adults aged 18 and older and is not directed
to children. This Android audience replaces the general-audience description
in section 7 for the Google Play version. The game and its progress reports
provide practice feedback, not a medical diagnosis or treatment.
