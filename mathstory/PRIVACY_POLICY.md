# MATHSTORY — PRIVACY POLICY

**Effective Date:** May 15, 2026
**Last Updated:** May 15, 2026

---

## Introduction

MathStory is a mobile application that teaches math through interactive stories set in a fictional kingdom. The app is designed to be welcoming to learners of varied ages, including younger users.

Contact: **cankatsarac@gmail.com**

---

## 1. AGE NOTICE — IMPORTANT

MathStory may appeal to children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13 (or under 16 in the EEA without verifiable parental or guardian consent), in line with the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Article 8 GDPR.

To support this commitment:

- MathStory **does not require an account or any personal information** to play.
- MathStory **does not collect** names, ages, email addresses, photos, voice, or location.
- MathStory **does not show third-party advertising** and does not contain in-app links to external commerce or social platforms beyond the platform store.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information to us (which we do not collect, but could in theory be entered as free-text), please contact us at cankatsarac@gmail.com and we will assist in removing it. Because all data is stored locally on your child's device, you can permanently delete it by uninstalling the app.

---

## 2. WHAT WE COLLECT

### 2.1 Stored locally on the device

- Story progress, score, badges, and unlocked content.
- App preferences and onboarding state.

### 2.2 What we do NOT collect

- No account, no login.
- No name, age, email, phone number, contacts, photos, microphone, camera, or location.
- No advertising SDKs or advertising identifiers (IDFA / GAID).
- No third-party analytics SDKs.
- No backend operated by us.
- We do not sell, rent, or trade data.

## 5. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY (COPPA / GDPR Article 8)

We design MathStory to be safe for children:

- No personal information is required to use the app.
- No third-party data sharing.
- No behavioral advertising.
- No in-app purchases that target children with persuasive design.

If you are a parent or legal guardian and have questions about your child's privacy in MathStory, please contact cankatsarac@gmail.com.
