## 2. DESCRIPTION OF SERVICE

The Last Page is a mobile application that lets you:

- Capture a photograph of the last page of a book.
- Identify the book automatically using an AI vision service (Section 7) and canonical metadata from Open Library.
- Search for books manually by title, author, or ISBN.
- Save the closing line ("sentence") and a private reflection ("feeling") for each book.
- Browse your personal shelf of endings.
- Receive an optional, biweekly local reminder.
- Subscribe to The Last Page Premium for expanded capacity and additional features.

The Last Page does not provide a social network. There is no feed, no follower system, and no public sharing. Your shelf is private to your device.

## 7. AI BOOK IDENTIFICATION

The book-identification feature uses an external AI vision model (currently Google Gemini 2.5 Flash, accessed through the Rork Toolkit endpoint) to estimate a book's title and author from a photograph of its last page.

You acknowledge and agree that:

- Identification is best-effort and may be incorrect, partial, or unavailable.
- The model is given strict instructions to return only `{title, author, confidence}` and to refuse uncertain identifications.
- The photograph of the last page is the only piece of in-app data transmitted by this feature. Your sentence and feeling are never sent. See Privacy Policy, Section 4.1.
- The underlying model provider may operate from a jurisdiction outside your country, and HTTP-level metadata (such as IP address) is necessarily visible to the receiving server.

If you are uncomfortable with this trade-off, use the manual title-search flow, which does not invoke the AI vision model.

We may change the AI provider, model version, or endpoint at any time without notice, provided the privacy guarantees described in the Privacy Policy continue to apply.
