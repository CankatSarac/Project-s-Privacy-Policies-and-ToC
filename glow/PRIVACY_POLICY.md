# GLOW — KINDNESS MAP — PRIVACY POLICY

**Effective Date:** May 15, 2026
**Last Updated:** May 15, 2026

---

## Introduction

Glow — Kindness Map is a mobile application that helps you record and reflect on small acts of kindness, optionally tied to a place on the map.

Contact: **cankatsarac@gmail.com**

---

## 1. WHAT WE COLLECT

### 1.1 Stored locally on your device

- Acts of kindness you record (text, optional photo, optional location tag, optional timestamp).
- Map preferences and onboarding state.
- Notification opt-in flag (if applicable).

### 1.2 Optional location

If you grant location permission, Glow may read your device coordinates to tag a kindness act with a place. Coordinates are stored on your device with the entry. They are not transmitted to any backend operated by us.

### 1.3 Optional photo

If you grant photo-library permission, you can attach a photo to a kindness entry. The photo file is stored on your device. It is not transmitted to any backend operated by us.

### 1.4 Information we do NOT collect

- No account, no login.
- No name, email, phone number, contacts.
- No microphone or camera (we use the photo library picker only).
- No advertising SDKs or identifiers.
- No third-party analytics SDKs.
- We do not operate a backend that holds user data.
- We do not sell, rent, or trade data.

## 5. YOUR RIGHTS

- **Access:** all data is on your device.
- **Delete:** uninstall the app, or use in-app delete.
- **Withdraw consent for location/photos:** revoke permissions in OS settings.
- **EEA / UK:** lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority.
- **California (CCPA / CPRA):** know, delete, correct, opt-out of sale/sharing (we do not), limit sensitive PI use, non-discrimination.
