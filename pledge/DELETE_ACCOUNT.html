Delete your Pledge account or data

Pledge, also known as AIPledge, is provided by Cankat Sarac.

Request deletion without the app

Email cankatsarac@gmail.com with the subject “Pledge account or data deletion”. You do not need to reinstall the app or sign in to send a request.

Tell us whether you want to delete your account or request deletion of particular service data. Include the email used for your Pledge account. For purchase-service requests, include your app user ID or store order reference if available. Do not send your password, payment-card details, or private pledge content.

To protect your account, we may ask you to verify ownership. We will explain which records are covered by your request and any retention limits.

Delete your account in the app

Sign in, open Profile, choose Delete account, and confirm. You may need to sign in again for security. A successful in-app deletion removes your Firebase sign-in account and associated legacy cloud account records.

Local data, copies and backups

Your device’s local pledges, tasks and progress are separate from account deletion. Use the app’s data controls or your device’s app-data controls to remove local app data.

Exported copies, backups and data on paired devices are separate. Manually remove copies and backups you control, and use the relevant app or device controls to remove data on other devices. Removing the app does not guarantee removal of these separate copies.

Other service records and subscriptions

In-app account deletion does not automatically delete purchase-service profiles, external AI-provider records or store transaction records. Use the email request above for other service data. Records that providers must keep for legal, security or transaction purposes may be retained under their policies; we will explain applicable limits when responding.

Subscriptions are managed separately through the store where you purchased them. Use your Apple or Google subscription settings to manage a subscription.
