# THE STRANGER APP PRIVACY POLICY

**Effective Date:** May 12, 2026
**Last Updated:** May 12, 2026

## Introduction

Welcome to The Stranger ("we," "our," or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use The Stranger mobile application (the "App").

The Stranger is a 365-day daily habit that expands empathy through intimate, data-grounded portraits of real global lives. Each day, the App shows you a portrait of one human being somewhere on Earth and a single comparison line that places your life next to theirs. The App is designed to work entirely offline. There is no account. There is no server that knows you exist. Everything you tell the App stays on the device in your hand.

## 1. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

### 1.1 Information You Provide Directly

During onboarding, you may optionally answer a small number of questions about yourself:

- Age band (e.g., 25–34, 35–44)
- Country of residence (from a fixed list)
- Approximate annual income band (e.g., $5–15k, $15–50k)
- Life stage (e.g., Student, Parent, Retired)
- Gender (optional)
- Education level (optional)
- Household size (optional)

Every one of these questions is optional. You can skip any or all of them. The App functions even if you skip everything.

We do not ask for your name, email, phone number, photograph, or any other identifying information. There is no account creation flow.

### 1.2 Information Stored Locally on Your Device

The following data is stored only on your device, in local app storage, and never transmitted to us or any third party:

- The onboarding answers you provided (Section 1.1)
- The list of stranger profiles you have viewed (your "archive")
- Your current and longest daily streak
- The day index for the rotating 365-day sequence
- Your language preference
- Your notification preference (on/off)
- An onboarding-completed flag

This data is held in the device's standard local storage (AsyncStorage on iOS and Android). It is removed when you uninstall the App, when you tap "Erase everything" in Settings, or when you clear the App's local data through your operating system.

### 1.3 Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect:

- Names, emails, phone numbers, or other identifiers
- IP addresses, advertising identifiers, or device fingerprints
- Precise or coarse location data (we never request location permissions)
- Photos, contacts, microphone audio, camera images, or files from your device
- Analytics events, screen views, taps, dwell time, scroll depth, or any behavioral data
- Crash reports tied to your identity (if we add crash reporting in a future version, it will be aggregate and opt-in)

The App contains no analytics SDK, no advertising SDK, no tracking pixels, and no data brokers.

### 1.4 Location Information

The App does not request location permissions and does not access GPS, Wi-Fi-based location, or cellular location. The "country of residence" you may select during onboarding is the only location-adjacent information ever recorded, and it is stored only on your device.

The stranger profiles include coordinates of the regions they depict so the App can show a small map dot. Those coordinates describe the stranger's region, not yours.

## 2. HOW WE USE YOUR INFORMATION

The information stored on your device is used solely to:

- Generate the daily counterfactual comparison line — the single sentence that imagines what your life would look like had you been born where the stranger lives
- Personalize the framing of insights (e.g., same-country comparisons, gendered comparisons where you have provided that data)
- Remember which strangers you have already seen and which day of the journey you are on
- Schedule the daily reminder notification at the time you choose
- Display your previous strangers in the archive

Because the data does not leave your device, we cannot use it for anything else. We have no way to read it, export it, sell it, or share it.

## 3. INFORMATION SHARING AND DISCLOSURE

### 3.1 We Do Not Sell or Share Your Data

We do not sell, rent, share, trade, or otherwise transfer your personal information to any third party. There is no business arrangement under which your information could be transferred, because no information ever reaches us.

### 3.2 Third-Party Services Embedded in the App

The App is built using:

- **Expo / React Native (650 Industries, Inc.)** — the framework used to build the App and ship updates. Expo's Over-the-Air update service (if enabled in future versions) downloads JavaScript bundles to your device. It does not receive your onboarding answers, archive, or any other personal data. It may log standard download events (App version, platform) that do not identify you.
- **Apple, Inc.** — App Store distribution. Apple receives the data described in its own App Store and Apple ID privacy policies, independent of us.
- **Google LLC** — Google Play Store distribution. Google receives the data described in its own Google Play and Google Account privacy policies, independent of us.

We do not embed Firebase, Sentry, Mixpanel, Amplitude, Google Analytics, Facebook SDK, or any similar third-party data service inside the App.

### 3.3 Push Notifications

If you opt in to the daily reminder, the App schedules a single local notification on your device through the operating system's notification framework (Apple Push Notification Service on iOS, Firebase Cloud Messaging on Android — both invoked locally as device-scheduled notifications, not as server-sent push). The reminder fires from your device. No server is involved. We never send you a notification from our side.

### 3.4 Sharing the App

The App includes a "Share this stranger" button that produces a text snippet or image card and hands it to your operating system's standard share sheet. You then choose whether and where to share it. The content you share contains no information about you — only the stranger's region, age, summary, and the counterfactual line.

### 3.5 Legal Requirements

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing we can disclose in response to a subpoena, warrant, or government request other than this Privacy Policy itself and our public source materials.

### 3.6 Business Transfers

If The Stranger is acquired, merged, or its assets are sold, this Privacy Policy will continue to apply to any data we hold at that time. Given that we hold no personal data, a business transfer cannot transfer your data.

## 4. DATA SECURITY

The data stored on your device is protected by the security features of your device and operating system:

- iOS sandboxes the App's storage so that only the App can read it
- Android sandboxes the App's storage similarly
- If your device has a passcode, biometric lock, or full-disk encryption, that protection extends to the App's local data

We do not transmit your onboarding answers over the network, which removes an entire category of risk.

No system is perfectly secure. If your device is compromised, locally stored data may be accessible to the attacker.

## 5. DATA RETENTION

Your local data is retained until one of the following events:

- You tap **Settings → Erase everything** and confirm
- You uninstall the App
- You clear the App's storage through your operating system's Settings → Apps menu
- You move to a new device without restoring App data from backup

We have no copy of your data and therefore have nothing to retain or delete on our side.

## 6. YOUR PRIVACY RIGHTS

### 6.1 Rights Under GDPR (European Economic Area, United Kingdom, Switzerland)

You have:

- The right to access the personal data held about you — exercisable by opening the App and reviewing the values in Settings → Your data
- The right to rectification — exercisable by tapping Settings → Redo onboarding
- The right to erasure — exercisable by tapping Settings → Erase everything
- The right to data portability — the data is small, structured, and viewable in Settings; there is no separate export needed
- The right to object to processing — by not using the App or by skipping any onboarding question
- The right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority

Because we never receive your data, we cannot act on these rights on a server. They are all exercisable in the App.

### 6.2 Rights Under CCPA/CPRA (California)

You have:

- The right to know what categories of personal information are collected — described above in Section 1
- The right to delete personal information — exercisable in Settings
- The right to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information — we do not sell or share, and never have
- The right to correct inaccurate personal information — exercisable by redoing onboarding
- The right to limit the use of sensitive personal information — we do not collect any of the categories California defines as "sensitive personal information"
- The right to non-discrimination for exercising your rights — the App functions identically whether you answer the onboarding questions or skip every one

### 6.3 How to Exercise Your Rights

Open the App. Go to **Settings**. Your data is on that screen. The actions you can take are on that screen. There is nothing for us to do on a server because there is no server.

If you have a question that cannot be resolved in the App, contact us at the address in Section 11.

## 7. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

The Stranger is not directed to children under 13 (or 16 in the European Economic Area, or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction). The App contains portraits of adult lives across the world — including descriptions of poverty, mortality, labor, and the texture of difficult ordinary days. Children should not use the App without a parent or guardian.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the applicable minimum age. Because the App does not transmit any user input off the device, even if a child were to use the App, no personal information would reach us.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has used the App and you wish to clear that usage, you can do so in **Settings → Erase everything**.

## 8. INTERNATIONAL DATA TRANSFERS

The App does not transfer personal information across borders, because the App does not transmit personal information at all. Your data remains on the device you are holding.

App distribution itself is handled by Apple and Google, who operate globally and may transfer the metadata they collect (purchase history, App download events) across borders under their own privacy policies.

## 9. THIRD-PARTY CONTENT INSIDE THE APP

The stranger profiles inside the App are crafted by us to be **real in their numbers and composite in their names**. Income figures, life expectancy, commute statistics, household composition, and regional context are grounded in publicly available demographic data (national censuses, World Bank indicators, OECD reports, UN agencies). The names attached to portraits are not the names of real, identifiable people. Any resemblance to a specific individual is unintended.

The App does not link out to third-party content during normal use. The "Share this stranger" feature hands a payload to your operating system's share sheet, which you may then send through any third-party application of your choice. That application's privacy policy then applies to the content you share through it.
