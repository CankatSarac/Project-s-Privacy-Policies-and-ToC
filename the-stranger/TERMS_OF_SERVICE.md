## 2. DESCRIPTION OF SERVICE

The Stranger is a mobile application that delivers a 365-day journey of one daily portrait. Each day, the App shows the user:

- A composite portrait of one human being somewhere on Earth
- A short "counterfactual" line that compares the user's life with the stranger's
- An archive of strangers the user has already met
- An optional daily reminder notification

All core features are free to use. The App contains no advertising, no paid tiers, no in-app purchases at the time of this writing. Portraits inside the App are crafted by us, grounded in publicly available demographic data, and are designed to be **real in their numbers and composite in their names**.
